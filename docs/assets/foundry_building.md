# Foundry building

Original 12 × 10 m industrial office, three 3.2 m floors. +Y entrance, +Z up in
Blender. Shared warm mineral, charcoal, blue glazing and restrained cyan markers.
Rhythmic facade bays, a centered entrance canopy, floor ledges, corner columns,
service panel and two roof HVAC assemblies. Closed shell, no usable interior yet.
Compact static collision. Under 20k triangles/eight materials. Seed 14721.
Recipe parameters and placements are recorded, so determinism is tested separately
from byte-for-byte Blender serialization. Godot mesh LOD is available; distant
building recipes deliberately omit facade detail. No copyrighted design or branding.
