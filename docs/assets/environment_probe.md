# Industrial environment probe

Original 8 × 6 meter floor and 8 × 3 meter wall, separate compact static collisions.
Blender +Y forward / +Z up; meters; origin at floor center. Three reused materials,
under 2,000 triangles. Concrete uses 4K basecolor, authored periodic tangent normal
and constant roughness .82; dielectric metallic 0. No inferred AO or displacement.
Two meter physical UV repeat. Graphite steel and cyan emitters are authored PBR.
The AI color contains panel joints; these are appearance only and have no depth.
This is a reusable quality sample, not a building or gameplay environment.
